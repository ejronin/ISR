# Iran War Public Evidence Atlas

Public-source OSINT atlas covering the 2026 Iran war, including verified damage, operational effects, bargaining chronology, claim checks, missile/drone performance, regional security alignments, China/Arctic shipping routes, and a GCC-vs-Iran economic outlook comparison.

## Evidence standard

- Physical damage, operational effect, actor claims, and analytic assessment are kept separate.
- Weak or disputed evidence is labeled as such.
- Published transport corridors are shown schematically; they are not live AIS tracks.
- Military locations are limited to public/coarse OSINT and historical evidence.
