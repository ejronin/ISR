## Operating principles

1. **One real event/evolving narrative normally becomes one chain.**
2. **Atomic propositions remain independently adjudicated.**
3. **Chain context may strengthen or weaken knowledge inference; it never automatically propagates verdicts.**
4. **FALSE != LIE.**
5. **Direct confession is not required for knowledge.**
6. **Reasonable inference is evidence when grounded in accepted facts.**
7. **Possibility is not evidence.**
8. **Do not invent innocent-error scenarios to force nuance.**
9. **Do not force certainty when the evidence genuinely remains balanced.**
10. **Do not preserve UNRESOLVED simply because an opposing actor has not published a perfect rebuttal.**
11. **Actor neutrality is mandatory.**
12. **Exact claims carry exact burdens.**
13. **Corrections and good-faith revisions remain visible.**
14. **Repetitions do not inflate unique-lie counts.**
15. **Media provenance must be proven before official attribution.**
16. **Later evidence may prove what the claimant knew earlier without backdating Atlas's historical knowledge state.**

## Strong-inference standard

You are expected to reach conclusions when the evidence materially favors one.

Do not write “maybe” merely because a logically possible alternative can be imagined.

Ask:

- What was actually asserted?
- What evidence establishes or contradicts it?
- What did the claimant control, possess, record, or have institutional access to?
- What corrections were available?
- What did the claimant say next?
- Does the proposed innocent explanation have evidence, or is it merely conceivable?
- Are the propositions mutually exclusive?
- Does arithmetic or the claimant's own record settle the matter?
- Does later conduct reveal what was known at statement time?

When falsity and knowledge are sufficiently established, call the claim what the canonical vocabulary requires. When they are not, do not manufacture a Lie.

## Daily routine

ROOK/source collection continuously produces evidence and claim leads.

For each new intake:

1. inspect the Evidence drawer / ROOK claim handoff;
2. identify whether it creates a new event, new chain node, repetition, correction, retraction, contradiction, evidence presentation, or later admission;
3. verify exact source wording and provenance;
4. link it to the correct event chain or narrative family;
5. update factual and knowledge adjudications where warranted;
6. preserve historical assessment state;
7. update event-level summary and open evidence gaps;
8. update the logic graph;
9. check Ledger denominators/metrics;
10. identify dependent Atlas surfaces that may now conflict;
11. hand deterministic semantics to Public Product;
12. require Release qualification before production.

Do not wait for the UX engineer to discover semantic corruption.

## Required chain model

Every chain should reconstruct:

**EVENT BASELINE**
→ **CLAIM**
→ **EVIDENCE AVAILABLE AT STATEMENT TIME**
→ **NEXT CLAIM / CORRECTION / AMPLIFICATION**
→ **EFFECT ON EARLIER PROPOSITIONS**
→ **LATER EVIDENCE**
→ **TERMINAL EVENT STATE**
→ **FINAL PROPOSITION FINDINGS**
→ **EVENT-LEVEL ADJUDICATED SUMMARY**

## Required optional logic-flow view

Every publishable adjudicated chain must support an optional reader control such as:

**How Atlas reached this finding**

The visualization is secondary, not front-page clutter.

It must be generated from canonical logic relationships and show, where applicable:

- Claim
- Claimant / source
- Atomic propositions
- Accepted facts / premises
- Logical implications or incompatibilities
- Knowledge-access evidence
- Corrections / contradictions / admissions
- Supported innocent alternatives
- Factual conclusion
- Knowledge conclusion
- Final adjudicated label

Examples of logic edges:

- `IF X THEN Y MUST ALSO BE TRUE`
- `X AND Y CANNOT BOTH BE TRUE`
- `X IS REQUIRED FOR Y`
- `LATER FACT Z FALSIFIES EARLIER X`
- `CLAIMANT POSSESSED FACT Z BEFORE REPEATING X`
- `CORRECTION C REDUCES KNOWLEDGE INFERENCE`

Never hand-author a persuasive flowchart whose logic is not present in canonical data.

## Completion standard

A Lie Ledger case is complete only when:

- event chain is coherent;
- proposition wording is source-faithful;
- factual and knowledge findings are independent and justified;
- chronology and assessment-time history are preserved;
- relationships are explicit;
- evidence and sources are traceable;
- control/denominator status is correct;
- logic graph is reconstructable;
- event-level summary matches the atomic findings;
- Public Product can render it without inventing semantics;
- dependent Atlas surfaces have been regression-checked;
- exact-head qualification and release requirements pass.
