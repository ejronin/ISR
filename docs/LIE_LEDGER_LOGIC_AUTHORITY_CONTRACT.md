# ATLAS LIE LEDGER — LOGIC, AUTHORITY, AND NON-DRIFT CONTRACT

**Status:** CONTROLLING LIE LEDGER GOVERNANCE  
**Owner:** Information Claims & Forensic Adjudication Engineer  
**Effective:** 2026-09-18

## Purpose

The Lie Ledger is a forensic claim-adjudication system, not a collection of isolated statements and not a rhetorical scorecard.

Its canonical unit is an **event/narrative chain containing independently adjudicated atomic propositions**. The record must preserve enough logic, chronology, provenance, and knowledge evidence that the public Lie Ledger can be rebuilt from canonical evidence without relying on remembered prose.

## 1. Top-level unit: event chain

The normal top-level unit is one underlying event or genuine evolving narrative.

Examples include:

- F-15E shootdown → aircraft identification → aircrew status → CSAR → purported physical evidence → rescue outcome → uranium/nuclear explanation;
- Turkey missile incident → denial → repeated denial → contrary attribution → false-flag substitution;
- F-35 combat damage → destruction/downing claims → later repetition;
- Qatar pilot losses → secret-custody allegation → counterchronology → obstruction allegation;
- USS Abraham Lincoln attack claim → damage/non-operational/retreat claims → later operational evidence.

Claims that merely share a rhetorical technique but concern separate events belong to a `narrative_family`, not one event chain.

## 2. Atomic propositions remain independent

Chain membership supplies context. It never propagates truth, falsity, knowledge, correction, or Lie status.

An event may contain, simultaneously:

- a supported occurrence;
- a false identity claim;
- an unresolved personnel claim;
- a misleading effect claim;
- a knowing falsehood.

The parent chain explains the story. Each proposition keeps its own adjudication.

## 3. Required evidentiary sequence

Every qualifying chain must be reconstructable as:

**EVENT REALITY**  
→ **CLAIM 1**  
→ **EVIDENCE AVAILABLE AT CLAIM 1**  
→ **CLAIM 2 / CORRECTION / AMPLIFICATION**  
→ **EFFECT ON EARLIER CLAIMS**  
→ **ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE**  
→ **TERMINAL FACTUAL STATE**  
→ **FINAL PROPOSITION ADJUDICATIONS**  
→ **EVENT-LEVEL SUMMARY**

The event-level summary never replaces proposition-level findings.

## 4. Truth and knowledge are separate questions

Factual outcomes may include:

- `SUPPORTED`
- `PARTLY_SUPPORTED`
- `UNRESOLVED`
- `UNSUBSTANTIATED`
- `MISLEADING`
- `FALSE`
- `FALSE_ATTRIBUTION`

Knowledge outcomes may include:

- `NOT_ASSESSABLE`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
- `POSSIBLE_KNOWLEDGE`
- `LIKELY_KNEW_FALSE`
- `VERY_LIKELY_KNEW_FALSE`
- `KNOWING_FALSEHOOD_ESTABLISHED`

**FALSE != LIE.**

A lie finding requires knowledge evidence, but direct confession is not required.

## 5. Knowledge inference

The adjudicator considers, where relevant:

- physical evidence possessed by the claimant;
- operational command/control;
- own-force launch/BDA/personnel records;
- custody records;
- diplomatic correspondence;
- claimant-produced imagery/video;
- prior statements and later corrections;
- elapsed time and repetition;
- internal contradiction;
- later admission;
- operational behavior;
- mutually exclusive propositions;
- fabricated or falsely presented evidence;
- exact accounting implying an internal ledger;
- selective omission of directly possessed facts.

Credible innocent explanations may include fog of war, genuine BDA error, misidentification, reporting delay, stale intelligence, translation error, subordinate-source error, compartmentation, or incomplete information.

**Possibility is not evidence.** An innocent explanation receives weight only when the record provides a reason to consider it plausible.

## 6. Temporal self-falsification

Later evidence may change the current adjudication of an earlier statement without pretending Atlas possessed that later evidence at the earlier time.

Preserve:

- assessment at statement time;
- later evidence;
- current adjudication.

The rule is:

> The lie, if any, occurs when the knowingly false statement was made. Later evidence may be what allows Atlas to prove the earlier falsity and knowledge.

## 7. Claim mutation never erases prior claims

Materially distinct statements remain separate nodes.

Relationship types include at minimum:

- `ORIGINATION`
- `REPETITION`
- `AMPLIFICATION`
- `CORRECTION`
- `RETRACTION`
- `NARROWING`
- `CONTRADICTION`
- `NARRATIVE_SUBSTITUTION`
- `EVIDENTIARY_SUPPORT`
- `FALSE_EVIDENCE_PRESENTATION`
- `LATER_ADMISSION`

A correction does not delete the false original. A narrative substitution is not a correction unless the evidence establishes it as one.

## 8. Source precision

Atlas adjudicates what was actually said.

Do not strengthen a hedge or weaken a categorical claim after the fact.

Examples:

- “may have been captured” is not “was captured”;
- “hit 16 aircraft” is not “destroyed 16 aircraft”;
- “possibly a uranium operation” is not “the United States stole uranium.”

Exact counts, ordinals, identities, universals, casualty totals, destruction claims, and categorical attributions carry the burden corresponding to their precision.

## 9. Absence of evidence

No corroboration normally supports `UNSUBSTANTIATED`, not automatically `FALSE`.

Falsity may be established by affirmative contrary evidence, logical incompatibility, arithmetic impossibility, claimant self-contradiction, physical evidence, later admission, or another discriminating basis.

## 10. Claimant's own evidence may adjudicate the claim

Atlas may use claimant-produced material against the claimant's proposition.

Examples include:

- displaying F-15E-identifying debris while claiming an F-35;
- publishing a “210 downed” headline while the same article says “around 210 targeted”;
- presenting supposed remains later acknowledged as wrong.

Use `CONTRADICTED_BY_OWN_EVIDENCE` where appropriate.

## 11. Institutional knowledge

Do not assume every official knows everything known by the institution, but do not treat institutions as incapable of knowledge.

Assess:

- role and subject-matter nexus;
- control/custody of the contradictory evidence;
- whether the institution necessarily records the fact;
- elapsed time;
- repetition after correction opportunities;
- cross-channel official repetition;
- later institutional acknowledgment;
- how many independent failures an innocent explanation would require.

State whether knowledge is individual, organizational, or inferentially institutional.

## 12. Event-level outcomes are composite

A parent event may contain several different proposition results. The parent summary must explain the evolution without flattening every branch into a single badge.

## 13. Repetitions and metrics

Repetition or amplification normally does not create another unique lie. It may materially strengthen the knowledge finding.

Ledger metrics must distinguish:

- unique propositions;
- repetitions/amplifications;
- corrections/retractions;
- supported claims;
- false claims;
- lies;
- unsubstantiated claims;
- unresolved claims;
- control/non-accusation cases.

## 14. Corrections are visible evidence

Prompt good-faith correction may reduce the knowledge inference. Late correction after overwhelming contrary evidence may carry different weight. Corrections must never disappear because they weaken an accusation.

## 15. Controls are not accusations

Admission-delay, correction, supported-statement, and other comparison cases may remain as `CONTROL_CASE` or `NON_ACCUSATION_CONTEXT`, but must not inflate ordinary Lie Ledger accusation totals.

## 16. Rhetoric and policy statements

Political rhetoric, prospective doctrine, threats, and strategic characterizations require a separate denominator unless a clean factual proposition can be isolated.

Do not manufacture a lie from rhetoric merely because it is exaggerated.

## 17. Media forensics require provenance

False or recycled imagery is not automatically attributable to an official actor.

Distinguish official, state-media, semiofficial, anonymous, and third-party provenance.

Use a result such as:

`FALSE MEDIA ARTIFACT — OFFICIAL ORIGIN NOT ESTABLISHED`

when that is what the evidence supports.

## 19. Authority boundaries

### Evidence Integration

Owns the broader physical/factual war record: events, chronology, losses, facilities, casualties, economics, sources, canonical historical state, and correction history.

Evidence Integration supplies the event baseline and source material. Once a proposition enters the claim-information-war domain, claim adjudication authority belongs to Information Claims & Forensic Adjudication.

Evidence Integration does not independently promote a contradiction to `LIE`.

### Information Claims & Forensic Adjudication

Owns what was claimed, how it evolved, whether it was true, what the claimant knew or reasonably must have known, final claim adjudication, claim-chain structure, Lie Ledger wording, and Lie Ledger denominator membership.

Within the Lie Ledger domain, this lane is semantically autonomous from other engineering lanes. It answers to the Atlas Systems Lead for project priority, scope, and integration only. The Systems Lead does not rewrite case wording, chain structure, or adjudication merely by managerial preference; any substantive override must be evidence-based and recorded as an adjudication change.

### Public Product / UX

Owns visual design and implementation: layout, interaction, responsive behavior, accessibility, typography, drawers, navigation, animation, and integration into the public reader.

Public Product may not strengthen, weaken, relabel, regroup, or reinterpret Lie Ledger semantics. Lie Ledger wording is supplied by the Claims Forensics lane; Public Product implements it.

### Release Integrity

Owns deterministic preservation of canonical → generated → public semantics. Release does not adjudicate truth.

## 20. Required logic-flow artifact

Every publishable adjudicated case must have a machine-reconstructable logic graph and an optional reader view such as **“How Atlas reached this finding.”**

The logic graph is secondary/optional UI, not front-page clutter.

Minimum conceptual structure:

- **Claim:** exact or faithful claim wording;
- **Claimant:** named actor/source;
- **Direct propositions:** the atomic assertions embedded in the claim;
- **Premises / accepted facts:** evidence relevant to those propositions;
- **Logical dependencies:** e.g. “if X is true, Y cannot be true,” “if X is true, Y must also be true,” or “X and Y are mutually exclusive”;
- **Knowledge-access facts:** evidence bearing on what the claimant knew;
- **Corrections / later admissions / contradictions;**
- **Credible alternatives actually supported by evidence;**
- **Factual conclusion;**
- **Knowledge conclusion;**
- **Final adjudicated label.**

The public flowchart must be generated from canonical logic relationships. Do not hand-author a persuasive diagram disconnected from the evidence object.

## 22. Required public card structure

A normal chain card should support:

### WHAT HAPPENED
Established event baseline.

### WHAT WAS CLAIMED
Chronological claim chain.

### HOW THE STORY CHANGED
Corrections, contradictions, amplifications, and substitutions.

### WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS
Evidence resolving each proposition.

### ADJUDICATED OUTCOME
Plain-language event-level conclusion.

### CLAIM RESULTS
Independent proposition dispositions.

### HOW WE KNOW
Primary/high-value evidence.

### HOW THE LOGIC WORKS
Optional logic-flow visualization generated from canonical relationships.

### WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN
Actual unresolved evidence gaps.

## 23. Non-drift rules

Validation must fail if a change:

1. strengthens or weakens the source proposition without an explicit fidelity record;
2. changes `FALSE` to `LIE` without knowledge evidence;
3. removes a correction/retraction;
4. inflates counts using repetitions;
5. combines separate events because of shared rhetoric;
6. fragments claim mutations that belong to one event;
7. flips proposition polarity without corresponding adjudication repair;
8. marks a supported event fact false because another branch was false;
9. marks an entire article false when only one proposition was false;
10. treats no corroboration as automatic falsity;
11. invents an innocent explanation without evidence;
12. attributes third-party fake media to an official actor without provenance;
13. lets Public Product alter claim semantics;
14. changes denominator membership without explicit adjudication;
15. discards historical assessment state when later evidence changes current adjudication.

## 24. Cross-surface regression

After a Lie Ledger correction, identify every Atlas surface consuming the corrected proposition/event and check chronology, losses, aircraft records, casualties, facilities, maps, narrative summaries, metrics, and evidence drawers.

Remediate contradictions exposed by the claim correction, but do not use the Ledger mandate to rebuild unrelated Atlas systems.

## 25. Rook / daily operating loop

ROOK is upstream collection and analytical discovery.

Routine flow:

**ROOK/source intake → Evidence Integration updates the factual/event record → Information Claims & Forensic Adjudication reviews new claim-domain material and maintains the Lie Ledger → Public Product renders the accepted Ledger semantics → Release verifies preservation.**

The Claims Forensics engineer must routinely inspect new ROOK evidence-drawer/claim handoffs for:

- new claims;
- repetitions/amplifications;
- corrections;
- retractions;
- contradictory evidence;
- later admissions;
- evidence presentations;
- changes affecting existing knowledge findings.

The Ledger is maintained continuously rather than rebuilt only when Public Product notices a presentation defect.

## 26. Reference implementation

`CH-F15E-CSAR-URANIUM` is the reference chain for validating the architecture.

The rebuilt chain must be capable of representing the genuine shootdown, type identification/correction, aircrew status, capture/death claims where sourced, bounty/search behavior where sourced, purported evidence, CSAR, equipment losses and attribution, successful crew recovery, rescue-failure claims, uranium/nuclear substitution, final proposition adjudications, and terminal event assessment.

Missing statements remain explicit collection gaps. Memory is not evidence.

## 27. Governing principle

The objective is not to maximize the number of Lies.

The objective is to make every conclusion—especially a Lie finding—difficult to attack because the claim, chronology, contradiction, knowledge, correction, evidence, and logic are visible and reproducible.

**If the evidence changes, the adjudication changes. If the presentation changes, the evidence does not.**
