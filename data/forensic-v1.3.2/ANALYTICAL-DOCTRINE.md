# Superseding Analytical Doctrine — Inference and Cost Estimation — v1.3.2 analytical-final

Effective for this add-on version. Where this file conflicts with earlier add-on language, this file controls.

## Proposition boundaries

Keep event existence, attribution/cause, physical effect, functional effect, operational outcome and strategic outcome separate. A real strike does not automatically establish the claimed weapon, system kill, mission kill or strategic consequence.

## Inference

Direct proof is not required when independent evidence converges. Use claimant statements, independent observation, physical signatures, geolocation/timing, capability/opportunity, opposing-party acknowledgement or alternative explanation, later operational behavior and credible competing hypotheses. Non-denial alone is weak evidence.

## UNRESOLVED

`UNRESOLVED` is narrow: use it only when the evidence does not materially favor one live explanation. Residual uncertainty is compatible with `MOSTLY_CONFIRMED` or `PARTLY_CONFIRMED`.

## Estimative judgment

Every consequential proposition stores a separate analytic likelihood (`ALMOST_CERTAIN`, `VERY_LIKELY`, `LIKELY`, `ROUGHLY_EVEN`, `UNLIKELY`, `VERY_UNLIKELY`, `REMOTE`), confidence (`HIGH`, `MODERATE`, `LOW`), inference basis, credible alternatives, and evidence that would change the assessment. Controlled disposition and likelihood are not synonyms.

## Cost estimation

There is no permanent no-price state. During active research, a material-loss category may temporarily use `PENDING_ESTIMATE` only when no defensible bounded model has yet been completed and the missing inputs are explicit. In this release, all canonical Iran material-loss categories have bounded models. Permitted methods include procurement/contract costs, CPI-adjusted historical acquisition, contemporary same-class/capability analogues, component build-up, local reconstruction rates, engineering parametrics, defensible damage-severity ranges and transparent analyst ranges.

Calculated estimates are analytical products. Join supported quantities and defensible unit-cost bases transparently rather than waiting for a publisher to print the multiplication. Never manufacture false precision.

## Iran loss envelopes

For standing material loss/damage, use three separate scenarios: `CONSERVATIVE EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED FLOOR`, `CENTRAL MODELED ESTIMATE`, and `ASSESSED UPPER ENVELOPE`. Maintain the evidence basis inside each category; the low envelope may include intelligence assessments, combatant assessments, and bounded analyst severity assumptions and therefore is not labeled confirmed/observed. Munitions expenditure is a separate low/central/high accounting product using a common geographic/temporal scope. Do not combine standing material loss, destroyed inventory, and intentionally expended weapons without an explicit accounting scope and overlap test.

## Leadership

Senior Iranian leadership casualties are person-level canonical records, separated into senior political/state and senior military/security categories. Reconcile any legacy aggregate after itemization.

## Public wording

State the strongest conclusion the evidence supports. Do not convert reasonable inference into `UNKNOWN`, and do not convert physical damage into broader mission kill without evidence.


## v1.3 cost-completion implementation note

For the Iran loss model, `PENDING_ESTIMATE` is closed for every canonical material-loss category. Future refinements should update ranges/assumptions, not revert a completed category to no-price merely because precise itemized invoices remain unavailable. Hypothetical future repair/reconstitution spending is outside canonical current-loss accounting and must not be presented as current loss, damage, economic loss, or expenditure.
