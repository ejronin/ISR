## What changed in the claim-evolution addendum

The claim layer now decomposes compound narratives into **event existence / claimed cause / claimed effect / claimed outcome**. This prevents real Iranian successes or real U.S. losses from being used as automatic proof of stronger causal or strategic claims.

The F-15E/CSAR/uranium sequence is the model case. It preserves: Iran's real F-15E shootdown; the initial F-35 misidentification and later Iranian-source correction; provisional/capture reporting; Iranian fire hitting rescue helicopters; genuine U.S. aircraft/equipment loss; U.S. self-destruction of disabled equipment in the independent reconstruction; eventual recovery of both aviators; and the subsequent shift from “failed rescue” toward nuclear-raid/uranium explanations. The invariant political conclusion can therefore be graphed without flattening factual distinctions.

Other audited chains cover Kuwait friendly-fire attribution, Turkey launch denial/false-flag substitution, the damaged-vs-destroyed F-35 sequence, Project Freedom small boats, the Indian Ocean destroyer claim and recycled footage, aggregate aircraft/UAV kill claims, Qatar pilot detention, Bushehr aircraft claims, Al Udeid BDA, regional false-flag attributions, and Dena/Tangsiri admission latency.

In particular, Araqchi's Turkey false-flag warning is not rewritten as a specific accusation against Israel when the cited statement only says “enemies”/enemies of regional peace. No evidentiary verdict was silently changed merely to fit a narrative.
