### Missile/UAS inventory scope correction

Reuters (`SRC-08F854FB61B7`) says about one-third of the missile arsenal was certainly destroyed; another third had mixed status—damaged, destroyed, or buried/inaccessible—and some buried/damaged missiles could later be recovered. The prior upper model treated the entire mixed second third as full loss. v1.3.2 instead counts the certainly destroyed third plus a 50% replacement-equivalent physical-damage allowance on the ambiguous second third in the upper case. The remaining ambiguous/inaccessible share is non-additive capability degradation.

The prior 1,500 / 3,000 / 4,500 destroyed-UAS airframe-equivalent assumptions were removed. Reuters' drone statement is about roughly one-third of **capability**, not a physical prewar airframe inventory. No separate destroyed-UAS inventory dollars are now added, avoiding unsupported quantity precision and overlap with industrial-production losses.
